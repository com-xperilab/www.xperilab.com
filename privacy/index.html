Privacy

Xperilab apps do not collect your data. We run no servers that receive it, we have no accounts to sign up for, and there is no analytics, tracking, or advertising in any of our apps.

Effective 16 September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. We have no way to identify you and no record that you use our apps.

Where your data lives

Your data stays on your device and in accounts that belong to you. Specifically:

Elite Workouts

  • Workouts, routines, and history are stored on your device.
  • If you enable sync, that data is carried through your own private iCloud storage. Apple provides it, it is tied to your Apple Account, and we cannot read it.
  • With your permission, the app writes a summary of finished workouts to Apple Health. It does not read your health data.
  • Exercise video buttons open YouTube search directly. No request is made by us and no information about you is sent.
  • Shared workout links carry the workout inside the part of the web address after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so a shared workout is readable only by the person you send the link to.

Budge

  • With your permission, Budge connects to your Google account to read and write a spreadsheet you choose. Your data lives in that spreadsheet, in your Google account.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in your device's Keychain. They are never sent to us.
  • The app communicates only with Google, to sign you in and to read and write your spreadsheet.
  • Links to banks and brokerages open those companies' own apps or websites. Budge never asks for, sees, or stores credentials for them.

Other companies

Where an app uses Apple iCloud, Apple Health, or Google Sheets on your behalf, your use of those services is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies. We are not a party to that data and cannot access it.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date.
