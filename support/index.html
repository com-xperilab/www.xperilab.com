Support

Email support@xperilab.com and we will get back to you. There is no ticket system and no phone queue — a person reads it.

What to include

These make a problem far easier to find, so please include them if you can:

  • Which app, and the version and build number shown on its settings screen.
  • Your device and iOS version.
  • What you did, what happened, and what you expected instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Beta builds

Our apps are distributed through Apple TestFlight while in development. If TestFlight says a build has expired, the fix is usually to install the newest build from the TestFlight app. If none is available, email us and we will send an invitation.

Your data

We cannot look up your data to help you, because we do not have it — it stays on your device and in your own iCloud or Google account. See Privacy for what that means in each app.

Deleting your data

Deleting an app removes the data it stored on your device. Data synced through your own iCloud or Google account is yours to delete in that account. We hold nothing to delete on our side.
